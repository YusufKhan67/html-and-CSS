KidsCare Your health Guide

Your body is like a supercar the more you use it,the stronger it gets! Kids who move for60 minutes every day are happier,sleep better and do better in school.

Dancing, swimming, football, yoga anything that gets your heart beating faster counts! So get moving and have fun!

  • Eat a balanced diet with fruits and vegetables.
  • Drink plenty of water to stay hydrated.
  • Get enough sleep for your age group.
  • Wash your hands regularly to prevent illness.
