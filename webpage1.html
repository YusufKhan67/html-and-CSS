The Kingdom of Animals

What are animals?

Biologically speaking, an animal is a multicellular living organism that feeds on other organisms. The scientific study of animals is called zoology.

Key Characteristics

  • Multicellularity: No rigid cell walls like plants have.
  • Nutrition: Heterotrophic (must consume organic matter for energy).
  • Movement & Senses: Active locomotion and a nervous system.
  • Humans: Biologically classified as mammals within the animal kingdom.
